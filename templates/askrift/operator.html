Month-end report

One week before month-end, prepare the manual invoice list.

The export gives operator a copyable list for Kontó or another external invoicing system. Payment status stays manual until CEO later approves provider-backed payment automation.

Manual report checklist
  • Confirm customers marked pending, unpaid, or needs_review.
  • Confirm amount, billing month, and product/podcast relation.
  • Use net amount for Kontó line entry when VAT is added by the invoice tool.
  • Review gross, net, VSK, discount/free-time, and final billable amount.
  • Update manual status after external invoice/payment action.
