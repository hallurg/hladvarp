# Hladvarp.com Product Manifest

## 1. Mission

Hladvarp.com exists to make podcast publishing accessible, simple, open, and creator-owned.

Everyone should be able to start a podcast.

## 2. Vision

Hladvarp.com should become a trusted Icelandic podcast platform built around RSS, open standards, clear ownership, and fair rules for creators.

## 3. Problem

Podcast publishing can become too dependent on closed platforms, opaque monetization, confusing hosting terms, and technical barriers that keep ordinary people from starting.

Creators need a platform that helps them publish without taking ownership away from them.

## 4. Who It Serves

Hladvarp.com serves:

- new podcasters
- independent creators
- community projects
- organizations with audio publishing needs
- professional podcasts that need fair commercial tools

## 5. Who It Does Not Serve

Hladvarp.com does not serve people who want to manipulate attention, create clickbait, mass-produce low-quality content, hide sponsorships, or use the platform as a surveillance or data extraction tool.

## 6. Core Values

- Access
- Simplicity
- Creator ownership
- Open standards
- Fair commercial rules
- Trust
- Clear publishing

## 7. Product Principles

Free podcasts are welcome.

RSS and open standards matter.

Creators should understand where their content lives and how listeners can reach it.

The platform should make starting easy and publishing understandable.

Commercial-use rules should be clear, fair, and simple.

## 8. Business Principles

We do not charge people for starting.

We charge when podcasts become part of commercial activity or require professional/business features.

Commercial activity helps fund the platform.

Free podcasts may not include advertising, sponsorships, paid promotion, paid subscription revenue, or comparable monetization.

When a podcast becomes commercial, the responsible account should move to a paid plan.

## 9. Engineering Principles

Hladvarp engineering should protect RSS, feed stability, media availability, backups, and creator access.

Changes to publishing, feeds, login, media, or ownership must be reviewed carefully.

Feature enforcement should be centralized through policy helpers, not scattered through ad hoc plan checks.

## 10. Things We Will Never Become

- Advertising company
- Clickbait platform
- AI-generated content farm
- Surveillance platform
- Vendor lock-in platform

## 11. Definition Of Success

Success means a person can start a podcast without being blocked by cost, confusion, or closed-platform dependency.

Success also means commercial creators help fund the platform fairly when their podcast becomes part of business or monetization.

## 12. Long-Term Vision

Hladvarp.com should become a durable Icelandic home for podcast publishing, with fair creator terms, open distribution, and professional capabilities that grow without compromising access.

## 13. Relationship To Kaupfjelag Naersveitamanna

Hladvarp.com expresses Kaupfjelag's principle of helping people spend time on what matters by reducing friction around audio creation and publishing.

It must remain governed by the company principles of reliability, truth, transparency, respect, and long-term thinking.
