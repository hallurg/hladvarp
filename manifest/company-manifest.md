# Kaupfjelag Naersveitamanna Company Manifest

## 1. Mission

We help people spend time on what matters.

Kaupfjelag Naersveitamanna builds systems that reduce unnecessary complexity, protect trust, and support meaningful work.

## 2. Vision

The company should become a disciplined builder of reliable, transparent, human-supporting software for communities, creators, organizations, and professionals who need technology they can trust.

## 3. Problem

Too much software asks people to serve the system. It adds complexity, hides decisions, automates weak processes, and rewards activity over outcomes.

Kaupfjelag exists to do the opposite.

## 4. Who It Serves

Kaupfjelag serves people and organizations that need software to make work clearer, safer, more reliable, and more meaningful.

It serves users who value:

- reliability
- truth
- understandable decisions
- practical usefulness
- long-term trust

## 5. Who It Does Not Serve

Kaupfjelag does not serve products or customers that require deception, surveillance, exploitative lock-in, careless automation, or speed at the expense of human accountability.

## 6. Core Values

- Reliability
- Truth
- Meaningful outcomes
- Transparency
- Respect
- Long-term thinking

## 7. Product Principles

Products should remove unnecessary complexity.

Technology exists to support people, not the other way around.

Processes should become simpler, clearer, and more humane before they are automated.

Automation is valuable only when created through careful thought.

## 8. Business Principles

Business models must align with trust.

Revenue should not depend on confusing users, exploiting private data, hiding costs, or making exit difficult.

Pricing should be understandable, fair, and connected to real value.

## 9. Engineering Principles

- Documentation first
- Architecture before implementation
- Governance before automation
- Review before release
- Never automate poor processes

Engineering should create systems that can be understood, operated, reviewed, and safely changed.

## 10. Things We Will Never Become

- A surveillance company
- A dark-pattern product company
- A blind automation factory
- A vendor lock-in business
- A company that treats documentation as optional
- A company where speed overrides reliability

## 11. Definition Of Success

Success means users spend less time fighting tools and more time doing meaningful work.

Success means teams can explain what they built, why it exists, how it behaves, and what it must not do.

## 12. Long-Term Vision

Kaupfjelag should become a family of governed products with shared principles, reusable operating discipline, and clear product identities.

The company should grow by earning trust, not by creating dependency.

## 13. Relationship To Kaupfjelag Naersveitamanna

This company manifest governs every Kaupfjelag product. Individual product manifests may add domain-specific principles, but they must not contradict this company-level frame.
