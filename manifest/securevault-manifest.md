# SecureVault Product Manifest

## 1. Mission

SecureVault exists to protect confidential information.

SecureVault exists to protect information, not exploit it.

## 2. Vision

SecureVault should become a trusted confidential data vault for organizations and workflows where privacy, evidence, auditability, and security are not optional.

## 3. Problem

Sensitive information is often scattered across email, file shares, chat tools, insecure forms, and undocumented manual processes.

This creates risk:

- unclear access
- weak audit trails
- accidental exposure
- poor evidence handling
- compliance uncertainty

## 4. Who It Serves

SecureVault serves organizations and professionals that need to collect, store, review, and handle confidential data with discipline.

It serves teams that value:

- trust
- confidentiality
- auditability
- privacy
- compliance
- evidence
- security

## 5. Who It Does Not Serve

SecureVault does not serve organizations that want to harvest private information, bypass consent, hide access patterns, or treat security as cosmetic.

## 6. Core Values

- Trust
- Confidentiality
- Privacy
- Security
- Auditability
- Evidence
- Compliance
- Least privilege

## 7. Product Principles

SecureVault should make sensitive workflows safer and more accountable.

Users should know what information exists, who can access it, what changed, and why.

The product should reduce uncontrolled copying and informal handling of confidential data.

## 8. Business Principles

SecureVault's business model must align with protection, not exploitation.

Revenue must never depend on selling, mining, or repurposing confidential data.

Trust is the product's primary asset.

## 9. Engineering Principles

Security controls must be real, testable, and documented.

Access control, audit logging, backup, restore, encryption, and retention behavior must be designed before major implementation.

Sensitive data workflows require review before release.

## 10. Things We Will Never Become

- A data broker
- A surveillance system
- A dark-pattern consent system
- A storage bucket with security branding
- A tool that hides access from data owners
- A system that trades confidentiality for convenience without explicit governance

## 11. Definition Of Success

Success means confidential information is easier to protect, easier to audit, and harder to mishandle.

Success means users trust the system because its behavior is clear, controlled, and reviewable.

## 12. Long-Term Vision

SecureVault should become a reusable confidentiality foundation for Kaupfjelag projects and external customers that require careful information handling.

## 13. Relationship To Kaupfjelag Naersveitamanna

SecureVault expresses Kaupfjelag's commitment to reliability, truth, transparency, and respect by protecting information instead of extracting value from it.
