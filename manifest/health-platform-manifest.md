# Health Platform Product Manifest

## 1. Mission

The Health Platform exists to help people prepare better health conversations through evidence-governed navigation, transparent context, and patient-first ownership.

The platform prepares conversations. It does not replace them.

## 2. Vision

The Health Platform should support care journeys by helping patients, families, and professionals organize evidence, questions, context, and consultation preparation without pretending to be a clinician.

## 3. Problem

People often enter health conversations with fragmented information, unclear timelines, unmanaged questions, and uncertainty about what matters.

Healthcare professionals also need better context, but tools must not cross into diagnosis, medication direction, or unsupported treatment recommendations.

## 4. Who It Serves

The platform serves:

- patients preparing for conversations
- families supporting care journeys
- professionals reviewing structured context
- teams that need evidence-governed navigation support

## 5. Who It Does Not Serve

The Health Platform does not serve users seeking automated diagnosis, medication changes, treatment instructions, or a replacement for professional medical judgment.

## 6. Core Values

- Patient-first ownership
- Transparency
- Scientific integrity
- Evidence governance
- Care journey support
- Consultation support
- Safety
- Humility

## 7. Product Principles

The platform should help users organize information, prepare questions, understand context, and support conversations.

It must clearly separate preparation from clinical decision-making.

It should make uncertainty visible instead of pretending it does not exist.

## 8. Business Principles

The business model must not reward overclaiming, medical dependency, fear, or hidden influence.

Commercial incentives must never weaken scientific integrity or patient ownership.

## 9. Engineering Principles

AI boundaries must be explicit.

The system must not diagnose.

The system must not recommend medication changes.

The system must not act as a treatment engine.

Evidence, provenance, review state, and uncertainty must be represented clearly.

Medical/scientific governance must precede implementation.

## 10. Things We Will Never Become

- A diagnosis engine
- A medication-change engine
- A treatment engine
- A substitute for clinicians
- A fear-based engagement product
- A system that hides evidence quality or uncertainty

## 11. Definition Of Success

Success means users arrive at health conversations better prepared, with clearer context, better questions, and stronger ownership of their information.

Success does not mean replacing clinicians.

## 12. Long-Term Vision

The Health Platform should become a trusted evidence-governed support layer for care journeys, always bounded by transparency, scientific integrity, and professional accountability.

## 13. Relationship To Kaupfjelag Naersveitamanna

The Health Platform expresses Kaupfjelag's commitment to meaningful outcomes and careful automation by supporting people before and around clinical conversations without pretending to replace them.
